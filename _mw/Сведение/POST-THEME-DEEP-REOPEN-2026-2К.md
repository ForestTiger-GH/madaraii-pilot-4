## 1. Общая модель периода

II квартал 2026 года формирует не один банковский сюжет, а систему из нескольких связанных процессов.

**Макроэкономика:** экономика восстановилась после слабого I квартала, но рост был потребительско-сервисным, тогда как инвестиции и промышленность оставались слабыми. Ключевая ставка снизилась на 75 б.п., однако финансовые условия оставались жесткими и инфляционные риски — значимыми.

**Процентная экономика:** это создало временно благоприятную repricing geometry для многих банков: стоимость фондирования снижалась быстрее доходности активов. ЧПД вырос у большинства core/extended banks.

**Кредит:** корпоративный и розничный lending ускорились на уровне сектора, но individual banks выбрали разные стратегии — от rebound и rapid рост до deleveraging и ликвидность build.

**Риск:** рост кредита происходил одновременно с повышением проблемного stock сектора, рискованных реструктуризаций и корпоративных reserve expenses. На bank level ухудшение проявлялось через Stage 2, Stage 3, NPL amount, specific product vintages и concentration.

**Прибыль:** лучший interest core не означал лучший чистая прибыль. FV, reserves, операционные расходы, tax, insurance, hidden residuals и M&A effects materially определяли Q2.

**Капитал:** rapid рост banks быстрее наращивали активы, взвешенные по риску, чем capital; ratios могли снижаться даже при прибыль and absolute capital рост.

**Структурная трансформация:** platforms, M&A, securitization, alternative payment rails, technology and AI increasingly change how balance, client interface and фондирование are built. Эти механизмы важны даже там, где Q2 P&L effect остается UNKNOWN.

## 2. Главные cross-theme связи

### 2.1. T1 → T3: rate easing → ЧПД, but not прибыль

T1 establishes sector фондирование cost declined faster than asset yield. T3 confirms this at bank level. Strong evidence:
- Sber cost-side explains ~70,5% ΔЧПД;
- PSB ~89%;
- Alfa/RSHB same direction;
- Sovcom/MTS раскрытый чистая процентная маржа improvement.

But T3 prevents causal overreach: прибыль can fall despite this mechanism. VTB/GPB/TBank show основной доход up while FV/ECL/операционные расходы/tax reduce чистая прибыль.

**Reconciled claim:** Q2 rate/фондирование repricing supported ЧПД for many banks; it did not determine total прибыль by itself.

### 2.2. T2 ↔ T4: credit рост and риск are simultaneous

Sector корпоративный claims +~3,7% and розничный lending accelerated. At the same time:
- корпоративный problem share 11,6→11,8%;
- risky restructurings +10%;
- розничный problem share 6,1→6,3%;
- корпоративный reserve expenses rose strongly.

Bank examples: rapid Ozon рост + ECL/Stage3 pressure; GPB корпоративный рост + Stage2; Sber credit density + Stage3; DOM.RF рост + allowance; MKB weak balance + Stage3 deterioration.

**Reconciled claim:** рост and риск are not alternative narratives. They coexist, and business-model/product mix determines where риск surfaces.

### 2.3. T2 ↔ T7: stock is not origination

Securitization materially changes раскрытый кредиты. DOM.RF can originate mortgages while closing розничный stock falls. VTB/TBank use ABS for capital/balance management. Thus bank кредит рост must distinguish retained stock from transferred pools.

**Reconciled claim:** balance-sheet кредит stock is not sufficient to measure new business where securitization is material.

### 2.4. T2 ↔ T6: policy channels change credit allocation

Family Mortgage and state/guarantee programs sustain lending under high market rates. Project finance and state-linked borrowers help explain корпоративный credit рост despite weak aggregate investment.

**Limit:** program participation cannot be used to attribute each bank’s рост without actual issuance/portfolio evidence.

### 2.5. T5 ↔ T9: strategy is increasingly operationalized through technology

платформенный strategy requires APIs, payments, embedded finance, cyber and data. Ozon/WB/Yandex/TBank/RSHB examples show client-interface strategy and technology are one coupled system.

But T9 blocks the simplistic implication “digital strategy → lower current costs.” Ozon/TBank/Alfa can have rapid tech development and rising операционные расходы simultaneously.

### 2.6. T8 challenges T2/T3/T5

M&A changes perimeters and destroys naive q/q/y/y comparability:
- Sovcom insurers;
- T‑Tech Auto.ru;
- Alfa acquisitions/leasing;
- legal integrations VTB/MTS;
- pending MKB/DVB.

Sanctions also affect strategic/infrastructure routes but do not mechanically explain domestic прибыль.

**Reconciled claim:** before interpreting рост/прибыль as organic, period/периметр event status must be checked.

## 3. Apparent contradictions and their resolution

### 3.1. Sector прибыль down while ЧПД mechanics improve

Not contradiction. Sector base прибыль rose while net прибыль fell; reserve and non-base components worsened.

### 3.2. Lower NPL ratio with worse риск

VTB and Ozon show denominator dilution: absolute problematic stock can rise while ratio falls.

### 3.3. Reserve recovery with uncertain quality

RSHB: reserve recovery plus списания and missing gross stage stock. Accounting flow ≠ borrower health.

### 3.4. Capital grows while ratio falls

Ozon, DOM.RF, WB, TBank: активы, взвешенные по риску рост > capital рост. This is mechanical.

### 3.5. Credit stock falls while lending activity remains strong

DOM.RF: securitization transfers активы. Alfa: balance рост is ликвидность, not credit. Different mechanisms, no contradiction.

### 3.6. Sanctioned bank can grow domestically

Sanctions constrain external legal/payment/фондирование channels. Domestic ruble balance and прибыль can still grow. Both facts can coexist.

### 3.7. AI adoption high while productivity ambiguous

Raiffeisen engineering evidence: high adoption without systematic end-to-end delivery improvement. Sber/Alfa/RSHB report local/management effects. Adoption and productivity are separate constructs.

## 4. Bank archetypes after cross-theme fan-in

### Сбер — mature universal / strong interest core / rising stock риск

Flat balance, higher credit density, strong ЧПД repricing, large dividend, Stage3 deterioration, tighter фондирование cushion, high technology/AI penetration and active capital-market infrastructure. Main tension: operating strength vs риск/фондирование pressure.

### ВТБ — рост + weak прибыль conversion + tighter capital

Credit рост, customer-фондирование outflow, strong ЧПД/fees, FV drag, розничный риск pressure, securitization for capital management, Pochta integration and WB partnership. Main tension: рост ambitions vs legal-entity capital and earnings volatility.

### РСХБ — specialized deleveraging / reserve-dependent прибыль

Credit contraction, розничный-фондирование outflow compensated by public/other funds, improved ЧПД, МСФО reserve recovery, agricultural/state mandate and own vertical маркетплейс. Main tension: headline прибыль vs underlying credit/фондирование quality.

### Газпромбанк — корпоративный рост / Stage2 / wholesale dependence

Moderate рост, higher reserve/операционные расходы burden, stronger prudential capital, корпоративный/transaction/debt-market role, sanctions-adapted external channels. Main tension: franchise strength vs риск migration and фондирование mix.

### ПСБ — rapid rebound / state-платформенный model

Very rapid активы/кредиты/фондирование/ЧПД, high активы, взвешенные по риску рост, limited stage observability, state/OPK mandate, A7 cross-border infrastructure, digital-rights activity and market фондирование. Main tension: expansion speed vs риск/capital visibility.

### МКБ — weak earnings / корпоративный риск / transformation

Balance/фондирование contraction, корпоративный Stage3 deterioration, low ROA, retained earnings via no-dividend decision, planned DVB integration and industrial DCM. Main tension: de-risking and expansion occurring simultaneously.

### ДОМ.РФ — project рост / securitization / capital consumption

Fast кредиты, client фондирование, stable группа прибыль, higher reserves, securitization and state-development role. Main tension: рост capacity depends on capital recycling and риск transfer.

### Альфа-Банк — ликвидность build / strong ЧПД / Stage2 / expansion периметр

активы grow through ликвидность while кредиты decline; ЧПД strong, Stage2 higher, группа операционные расходы higher, M&A/leasing/AI activity. Main tension: capacity/opportunity vs credit migration and integration costs.

### ОТП — high розничный profitability / auto concentration / branch redesign

High Q2 ROA/ROE proxies, фондирование and auto рост, auto Stage3 pressure, retained prior-year прибыль, CareTech/digital distribution. Main tension: strong current economics vs product concentration/риск.

### Озон Банк — платформенный scale-up / fee-heavy / fast активы, взвешенные по риску

Very rapid активы/фондирование, high fee income, rising операционные расходы/reserves, capital ratios down from high base, платформенный-native model and growing Fintech риск. Main tension: рост monetization vs cost/риск/capital velocity.

## 5. Evidence dependence

Repeated facts across bank, news and thematic Results often share upstream sources. They are not counted as independent evidence simply because they appear in multiple Results.

Examples:
- WB–VTB partnership appears in strategy, маркетплейс, M&A, news and sanctions contexts: one event with multiple uses.
- DOM.RF securitization appears in bank financials, debt and securitization themes: same transaction lineage.
- Sber AI 550bn appears in news/AI/forecast: one management estimate.
- M&A effects appear in financial and event lanes: event establishes периметр; accounting Result establishes financial observation.

Science must keep these typed relations instead of multiplying source count.

## 6. What the combined corpus supports

1. Q2 repricing improved interest economics for many banks.
2. Credit рост returned but was uneven and often capital-intensive.
3. Sector and bank-level риск did not broadly normalize; some new vintages improved while stock problems increased.
4. прибыль quality varied sharply because noninterest/риск/tax effects dominated some banks.
5. Fast-growing banks can retain high capital ratios while experiencing rapid запас капитала consumption.
6. Securitization materially changes interpretation of кредит stocks and capital management.
7. State programs influence credit allocation but do not alone explain bank рост.
8. Platforms and technology are changing distribution/business models, but Q2 financial contribution is often unmeasured.
9. M&A and sanctions are essential comparability/context layers.
10. Missing disclosure is material epistemic information, not a zero.
