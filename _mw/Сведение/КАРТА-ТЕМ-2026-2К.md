## Theme Contracts

**T1.** Scope: Q2 macro facts, sector rate mechanics, bank interest-income/expense bridges. Non-scope: прогноз как факт; causal attribution без transmission evidence. Consumer value: объяснить, почему NII мог улучшаться при жестких условиях. Stop: mechanism bounded and alternatives retained.

**T2.** Scope: comparable stock growth, loan/funding proxies, wholesale funding, regulatory capital/RWA/liquidity. Non-scope: единый ranking при разных constructs. Required: perimeter, denominator, capital construct, off-balance limits.

**T3.** Scope: Stage/NPL/overdue/coverage/ECL/reserve flow, one-offs, fair-value/tax/OPEX effects. Non-scope: reserve flow = asset quality; aggregate ratio = uniform risk. Required: stock/share/denominator/product migration and earnings bridge.

**T4.** Scope: business-model differences and extended-17 structural signals. Non-scope: platform group disclosure = standalone bank. Required: scale/base effects, fee economics, disclosure bias, comparison universe.

**T5.** Scope: state-supported and sector-concentrated lending channels, especially mortgage/construction and other material programs. Non-scope: attribution of each bank's loan growth to policy without bank-level evidence.

**T6.** Scope: M&A/perimeter/sanctions/events that alter interpretation, reporting comparability or access. Non-scope: actor statement as realized economic effect. Required: event date, close status, reporting perimeter, post-period label.

**T7.** Scope: structural market/technology channels with Q2 relevance. Non-scope: program registration = funding inflow; announced tech = realized efficiency; ESG statement = financial effect. Required: implementation status and transfer limits.
